Client: Greenleaf Interiors (fictional) — 8-person interior design studio Date: EXAMPLE

Summary

Greenleaf spends significant weekly time on quotations, answering repeat client questions, and searching past project files. The best near-term wins are a quote-drafting assistant and a searchable knowledge base over past projects. Invoice processing is better served by off-the-shelf tools than custom AI.

Current State

  • Quotes built manually in Excel from past projects
  • Clients ask similar questions on WhatsApp/email (timelines, materials, pricing)
  • Project files scattered across Google Drive folders

Top 3 Opportunities

# Opportunity Approach Effort Risk Build/Buy
1 Draft quotes from a short brief using past quotes as reference LLM + structured template Medium Medium (price accuracy; human review required) Light build
2 Answer repeat client FAQs with drafted replies RAG over FAQ + policy docs Low Low Buy/configure
3 Search past projects ("show kitchens under budget X with oak") RAG over Drive files Medium Low Build or buy

Not Recommended (Yet)

  • Fully autonomous client chatbot: risk of wrong commitments on price/timelines.
  • Custom invoice AI: existing accounting software features are sufficient.

Data & Risk Notes

  • Keep a human approving every quote and client reply.
  • Remove client personal data before indexing documents.

Recommended First Step This Week

Collect the 20 most common client questions and answers in one document — this becomes the base for opportunity #2.

30-Day Roadmap

  1. Week 1: FAQ document + try a drafted-reply tool.
  2. Week 2–3: Organise Drive folders; pilot search over past projects.
  3. Week 4: Prototype quote-drafting template with 10 past quotes.
